# Data provenance

The eight images, `test_cases.json`, and `recorded_results.json` are copied
unchanged from the RynnBrain 1.1 authors' public repository:

- Repository: https://github.com/alibaba-damo-academy/RynnBrain
- Commit: `eef38357e48bb6b8a1358aade3054f9a16b1cbb1`
- Original path: `cookbooks/assets/contact_point_prediction/`

They are used here to replay the released contact-point cookbook with
deterministic decoding and to compare fresh generations with the authors'
recorded outputs.
